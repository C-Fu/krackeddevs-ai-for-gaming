# Game Design Document: Haze Fighter

## Overview
- Title: Haze Fighter
- Genre: Action Arcade
- Target Audience: High school students (example: Ali, 16, Alam Shah Science School, Shah Alam)
- Unique Selling Point (USP): "Battle haze in the school canteen with humor, fans, and dry ice bombs."

## Core Concept
- Player Role: Makcik canteen
- Gameplay: Use fans and dry ice bombs to clear haze before students arrive for breakfast.
- Win Condition: Clear all haze within the time limit.
- Tone: Humorous, cartoony, lighthearted.

## Mechanics
- Player controls a single character (makcik).
- Actions:
  - Aim and activate fan to blow haze particles.
  - Throw dry ice bombs to disperse haze clouds.
- Power-ups:
  - Mega Fan: Stronger airflow, clears haze faster.
  - Dry Ice Bomb: Area-of-effect haze clearing.
- Timer: Countdown until students arrive; haze must be cleared before time runs out.

## Environment
- Scene: School canteen only.
- Visual Style: Cartoony, exaggerated haze effects, playful animations.

## Progression
- Prototype Depth: One level only.
- Narrative: As time runs low, urgency increases — haze density rises, forcing quick action.

## Babylon.js Features
- Particle systems for haze and dry ice effects.
- Simple sprite/mesh for makcik character.
- Basic physics for fan airflow and bomb dispersal.
- Minimal UI (timer, haze meter, power-up icons).

## Accessibility
- Controls: Simple keyboard/mouse or tap (spacebar for fan, click for bomb).
- Market research: Tailored for casual play by high school students, easy to pick up.
