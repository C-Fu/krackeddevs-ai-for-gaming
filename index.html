Highway Legends • Point A to B Sprint

SmartTAG Booster Ramps: Hit green toll ramps for supersonic leaps across gantries.

Haze Fighter • Time-Attack Action

Industrial Blower [Space]: Direct aerodynamic wind currents to clear heavy haze particles.

Dry Ice AOE Bombs [E / Click]: Throw freezing bombs to shatter dense toxic smoke.
