FOUNDATIONAL DESIGN THESIS

A GAME IS "POINT A TO POINT B" + 1-2 USPS

Every successful game boils down to moving from Point A to Point B.
What makes it unforgettable are 1 or 2 Unique Selling Propositions (USPs).

1. Point A (Origin)

The starting state, location, challenge, or emotional baseline. Establishes the player's motivation and rules.

2. Point B (Goal)

The destination, score milestone, or story climax. Creates urgency, tension, and a clear win/loss boundary.

3. The 1-2 USPs

The mechanical or environmental twist. Without USPs, traversal is a boring chore. With USPs, it becomes addictive play.

WHY DO INDIE GAMES FAIL? - Ask the room: "How many of you started a game project and never finished?" - Usually 80% raise hands. - Why? Because people design "An open world MMORPG with survival mechanics and dynamic economy". - Mario Kart: Start line (A) to Finish line (B) + Powerup Weapons (USP). - Subway Surfers: Run from Inspector (A) to Max Distance (B) + 3-Lane Dodge & Train Vaulting (USP). - Keep it laser-focused.

PHASE 3 • 01:30 - 01:50

BABYLON.JS ARCADE VEHICLE PHYSICS

How to give a web game genuine weight, responsiveness, and speed sensation

1. Acceleration Curve

Non-linear torque: reaches 110 km/h in 3s; cruises at 180 km/h; uncapped to 240 km/h during SmartTAG boost.

2. Chassis Banking Tilt

rootNode.rotation.z = lateralTilt
Leaning 8° into sharp turns gives immediate visual feedback of tire grip.

3. Dynamic Camera FOV

Camera radius pulls back and FOV widens as speed rises past 140 km/h, amplifying the sensation of velocity.

The Chase Camera Fix (Key Lesson):

In Babylon.js, FollowCamera.rotationOffset = 180° is required to place the camera behind the vehicle looking forward along the +Z expressway. At 0°, the camera looks backward at the front bumper!

IMPORTANT BUG/FIX STORY: - Share the real debugging moment we had: "When we first loaded the game, we were staring at the front grille!" - Explain why: FollowCamera default 0 deg looks backward. Setting 180 deg puts it behind the spoiler looking down the highway. - Attendees love hearing real debugging insights because they encounter the exact same things in their own projects.

The Prompt to Build This:

"In src/traffic.js, implement an AI state machine:
When player presses Spacebar (high beams active) and is within 40m behind 
a vehicle in the right lane (x = 5.8):
1. Activate the AI car's left blinker mesh.
2. Wait 0.28s reaction time.
3. Smoothly lerp x from 5.8 to 0.0 (middle lane) over 0.7s.
4. Trigger sound.playYieldSuccess() and award +250 points!"
LIVE DEMO STEP: - Have attendees observe the traffic cars. - Show what happens when you tailgate without high-beaming: Collision crash! - Show what happens when you tap Spacebar: The car's left blinker blinks, it swerves to the left, and the banner "FAST LANE CLEARED!" pops up. - It is instant positive feedback.
