# Example Game Spec: Neon Kart 3D

*[Live Game Demo](../neon_kart_3d/neon_kart_3d_ai_for_gaming_live_workshop_demo.html) | [Game Folder](../neon_kart_3d/) | [3-Hour Runbook](../neon_kart_3d/ai_for_gaming_3hour_lab_runbook_and_templates.md)*

## 1. High Concept & Overview
- **Title:** *Neon Kart 3D*
- **Genre:** 3D Arcade Kart Time-Trial & Item Racer (Mario Kart Style)
- **Platform:** Web Browser (Three.js + Vite, 60 FPS, Zero Backend)
- **Target Persona:** Beginner to intermediate workshop attendees learning agentic game development.
- **Premise (Point A to Point B):** Complete 3 laps on a glowing cyber circuit track, hit boost pads, deploy tactical power-ups, and beat 2 AI rival karts.

## 2. Core USPs
- **USP 1: Mini-Turbo Drift Sparks (`Shift` / `Spacebar`):** Holding drift around sharp hairpins charges a 2-tier mini-turbo boost that surges forward upon release.
- **USP 2: Floating Item Boxes:** Rotating golden cubes grant randomized power-ups:
  - *Turbo Mushroom:* 3-second instant speed boost.
  - *Plasma Shield:* Deflects obstacles and rival bump collisions.
  - *Oil Slick:* Traps AI rivals behind you.
